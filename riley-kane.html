8-Week Pre-Release Content Calendar
WK -8 to -6
Snippet Testing Phase — Post 5x/week, test different 15–30 sec clips. Track which hooks get saved and shared the most. Keep shooting raw content.
WK -5 to -3
Emotional Story Phase — Attach personal narratives to snippets. "This song is about the night…" — pair authentic storytelling with the audio. Drive emotional connection before the drop.
WK -2 to Release
Seeding Phase — Send to 10–15 micro-influencers in your niche. Give them the unreleased audio. No brief, just vibe. Let them create organically.
RELEASE WEEK
Launch + Engage — Reply to all comments within first 2 hrs. Use duet feature to react to creator content. Go live the night of release. Fan engagement = algorithmic signal.
POST-RELEASE
Sustain — Weekly content tied to the record. Monitor what content type is driving streams. Double down on it. Keep replying, keep posting, keep seeding.

Best Practices — Expand to Review

▼
The 8-Week Rule Start content 8 weeks before release. The algorithm needs time to learn your audience. Don't rush the seeding phase.
Emotional Story + Snippet = 2–5x Higher Share Rate Pair every audio snippet with a personal story. Context drives emotional investment. Shares = free distribution.
Raw Phone Quality Outperforms Polished Production TikTok and Reels algorithms favor native-looking content. Overly produced clips get deprioritized. Authenticity wins.
Reply to Every Comment in the First 2 Hours Early engagement is the strongest algorithmic signal. Reply to comments, pin the best one, reply to your own pin. Force the loop.
